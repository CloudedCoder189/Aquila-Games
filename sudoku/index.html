How to play

Your goal is to fill every empty square with a number from 1 through 9.

The main rule: a number cannot repeat in the same row, column, or outlined 3 × 3 box.

The black numbers are clues and cannot be changed. Select an empty square, then choose the number you think belongs there.

If a number turns red, it repeats a number in the same row, column, or box. Use Erase or Undo and try another number.

A guess that does not create a conflict stays normal. The game will not reveal whether it matches the hidden solution.

Notes lets you add small candidate numbers without committing to an answer.

Check tells you whether the board is on the right track. Your progress is saved automatically.

Hard mode does not show conflicts or provide a Check button.

Use the Easy, Medium, and Hard buttons to switch between today's three puzzles.
