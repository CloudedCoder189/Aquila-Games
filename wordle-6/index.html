WORDLE UNLIMITED

Six-Letter Wordle

A new puzzle whenever you want

Made for the UPA community Unlimited puzzles

How to play

Guess the six-letter word in six tries.

Each guess must be a valid six-letter word. The color of the tiles changes to show how close your guess was.

PLANET

P is in the word and in the correct spot.

STREAM

R is in the word but in the wrong spot.

BRANCH

N is not in the word.
